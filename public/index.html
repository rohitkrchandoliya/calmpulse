Before anything starts: your camera, your choice

CalmPulse uses the Presage SmartSpectra SDK to estimate your pulse and breathing rate from your webcam, then guides a breathing exercise at a pace adapted to your measured breathing. Nothing is measured until you consent below and press Start. Please read how it works:

  • On your device. CalmPulse runs locally on this computer. When you start a session, the local CalmPulse process opens your webcam and the SmartSpectra SDK processes the video on this machine to estimate pulse and breathing.
  • No recording, no storage. Video frames exist only in memory while measuring. CalmPulse does not record, save, or upload video, and it does not store your readings. Closing this tab or pressing “Stop camera” ends measurement and releases the camera.
  • What leaves this machine. The SDK contacts Presage servers only to authenticate the API key and validate the subscription. CalmPulse disables SDK telemetry and never uses Presage's optional AI Insights feature, so no vitals are sent for analysis.
  • Wellness only. CalmPulse is a relaxation aid, not a medical device. It does not diagnose, treat, or monitor any condition, and its readings are estimates for personal wellness use.
  • You are in control. Consent is per-session. You can stop at any time, and nothing runs in the background.
